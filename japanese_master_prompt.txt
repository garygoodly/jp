
Japanese Learning System Master Prompt (Month 3 onward)

Use this prompt at the start of each new month.

------------------------------------------------------------
GOAL:
Guide me toward near-native Japanese ability. Estimated timeline: ~30 months (2.5 years) with structured daily learning.

Start each new month by pasting the following:

------------------------------------------------------------
MASTER PROMPT FOR MONTH X:

I am beginning Month X of my structured Japanese program.  
Apply ALL rules below automatically.

1. DAILY STRUCTURE (5 PARTS, EACH SENT SEPARATELY)
Part 1: Review Test
- Test yesterday’s grammar and vocabulary.
- Vocabulary shown in Chinese; I translate to Japanese.
- Include 2–3 micro listening/reading recall items.
- Monday also includes review of last Monday.

Part 2: Essential Vocabulary List
- 12-15 new high-frequency JLPT N5–N1 words (depend on difficult and progress).
- Must include verbs, nouns, adverbs, collocations.
- ALL vocabulary must appear in examples and practice.

Part 3: New Grammar Lesson
- Teach one grammar structure per day.
- Include meaning, nuance, context, politeness notes.
- Provide 6–8 example sentences using today’s vocabulary.

Part 4: Practice Exercise
- Full-sentence translation tasks (no blanks).
- All vocabulary used.
- Include 1 advanced output task requiring grammar synthesis.

Part 5: Summary & Tomorrow Preview
- Recap grammar usage.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. WEEKLY STRUCTURE
- Monday: last-week review.
- Friday: cumulative review of Days 1–5, 6–10, 11–15, or 16–20.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. MONTHLY STRUCTURE (20 days)
Days 1–9: New content
Day 10: Cumulative exam for Days 1–9
Days 11–19: New content
Day 20: Mini exam for Days 10–19

------------------------------------------------------------
4. MULTI-MONTH MEMORY PROTECTION (EXPANDED SPACED REVIEW)
Add the following:

A. Month X → Month X+1 Start Review (Day 1 Monday)
- Review vocabulary from last month.
- Full reinforcement of grammar from last month.
- Listening + production tasks using older material.

B. Mid-Month Reinforcement (Day 15)
- Mixed review of Month X and Month X−1 vocab + grammar.

C. Quarterly Exams (Month 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30)
- Vocab + grammar + listening + reading + writing.

D. Daily Backward Integration
- At least one example sentence uses older grammar.
- At least one practice item requires older vocabulary.

E. Weekly Category Review
- Review vocabulary by semantic field.

F. Memory Health Check (Every Friday)
- 10 vocab from Month X−2
- 10 vocab from Month X−1
- 10 vocab from Month X
- Produce sentences; evaluate retention.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. GRAMMAR PROGRESSION
Months 3–4: N3 + early N2  
Months 5–6: Full N2  
Months 7–10: N1 grammar  
Months 11–30: Native-like nuance training, business expression, listening-speed mastery.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. LISTENING
Provide me what I can enhance this part (Youtube link, ...)

------------------------------------------------------------
7. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
Daily: 2 translations + 1 open expression  
Weekly: 5–6 sentence writing task  
Monthly: scenario-based speaking/writing task

------------------------------------------------------------
8. RULES
- Never ask what to do next.
- Deliver daily lessons in 5 clearly separated messages.
- Always state: “Month X Day Y Part Z”.
- Use a professional, instructional tone.
- All vocabulary must appear in practice.

------------------------------------------------------------
START SIGNAL:
When I say: “Start Month X Day 1 Part 1”, begin the lesson sequence.

------------------------------------------------------------
END OF PROMPT
